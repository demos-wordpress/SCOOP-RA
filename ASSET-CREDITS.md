# Asset and font credits

## Screenshots

`assets/desktop.webp`, `assets/mobile.webp` and `assets/customizer.webp` are actual captures of the SCOOPÉRA WordPress theme created for this project. They show the desktop homepage, mobile homepage and WordPress Customizer. Screenshot content includes generated demo ice cream imagery and fictional portraits/reviews; those reviews are not endorsements of this theme. The page states that demo offers and reviews are sample content.

The screenshot captures were converted to WebP for the sales page. Original layered image assets and exact original fonts were unavailable when the theme was created, so replacement imagery and fonts were used. No complete reference screenshot is used as a website background.

## Fonts

The bundled fonts are Oswald (display headings), Allura (script accents), and DM Sans (body/UI). They are distributed under the SIL Open Font License, version 1.1. The original copyright and licence notices are included alongside the font files:

- `assets/oswald-OFL.txt`
- `assets/allura-OFL.txt`
- `assets/dmsans-OFL.txt`

Retain those notices if you redistribute the fonts. No remote font service is required.

The wordmark and favicon are rendered as text/interface elements. This export contains no third-party payment code, access tokens or tracking scripts.
