A few things to know.

BEFORE YOU
TAKE A SCOOP.

Is this a WordPress theme or an HTML template?

It is an installable classic PHP WordPress theme. Upload the ZIP through Appearance → Themes → Add New → Upload Theme, then activate it. The screenshots on this sales page show the actual theme.

Do I need a page builder or coding skills?

You can personalize the landing page through the native WordPress Customizer without editing code. Elementor, Divi, WPBakery and paid page builders are not required. Basic WordPress setup is still needed.

Can I accept orders and payments?

You can link order buttons to WhatsApp or an external ordering service. For an on-site shop, install and configure WooCommerce, add your products and set up your payment provider. WooCommerce and payment services are separate from the theme; payments are not preconfigured.

Are the images and fonts included?

Yes. The package includes locally hosted fonts and replacement demo imagery, with credits and licence notices. Images and portraits were generated for the demo. The preview reviews and offer are sample content; replace or hide them before promoting a real business.

Does the newsletter form work immediately?

Connect it to a hosted signup page or a supported form integration such as Contact Form 7, WPForms, MailPoet or Fluent Forms. You will need to configure your chosen service or plugin. The theme does not collect or silently discard addresses before setup.

What has been tested?

The theme was installed and activated in a real WordPress environment. Testing covered Customizer saving, flavour-card editing, section order, mobile navigation and layouts at 360, 390, 768, 1024 and 1440 pixels. WooCommerce cart and checkout rendering were also checked. Actual payments, newsletter delivery and every browser/device combination were not tested. The package includes the verification report.

Are hosting, installation or ongoing services included?

The $29 offer is for the downloadable theme package and its documentation. Hosting, a domain, installation services and third-party subscriptions are separate. No ongoing maintenance or lifetime update plan is included in this offer.
